Reality check on visas (2026): US H-1B sponsorship has become costly and unpredictable, so new sponsorship there is rare outside senior roles. The realistic relocation routes right now are Germany & the wider EU (Portugal, Poland, Netherlands), Japan, the UAE and Canada. "Relocation support" increasingly means moving help without a visa — always read the fine print for true sponsorship, and never pay a fee to apply.
